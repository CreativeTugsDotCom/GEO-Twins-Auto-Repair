WHAT WE DO

Complete Auto Care,
All Under One Roof.

From routine maintenance to complex mechanical repairs, our team helps keep your vehicle safe, reliable and road-ready.

Oil Changes & Maintenance

Keep your vehicle running smoothly with routine maintenance, synthetic oil changes, filter replacements, and scheduled factory service.

Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Accurate computer diagnostics and dependable repairs when your check-engine light comes on or performance degrades.

Brake Service

Complete brake inspections, pad and rotor replacements, caliper repairs, and fluid flushes to help keep you and your passengers safe.

Tire & Wheel Service

Professional tire rotation, wheel balancing, flat repair, and mounting for maximum safety, handling, and fuel efficiency.

Suspension & Steering

Repair worn shocks, struts, ball joints, control arms, and tie rods for a smoother, safer, and responsive ride across Brooklyn streets.

Heating & A/C

Keep your cabin comfortable year-round with heating and air-conditioning system inspection, refrigerant recharge, and compressor repair.

Cooling System

Cooling-system inspection, radiator repair, thermostat replacement, and coolant flushes to help prevent engine overheating.

General Mechanical Repair

Dependable mechanical repairs for a wide range of vehicle makes and models including alternator, starter, battery, and drive belt replacements.
